# NSDUH PCA content-group results

The complete-case analysis included 19,832 adults with past-year major
depressive episode, eight valid MDE symptom indicators, and four valid Sheehan
Disability Scale role-domain responses.

## Symptom contrast

The second component of the symptom-only PCA separated appetite/weight, sleep,
psychomotor, and fatigue indicators from depressed mood, anhedonia,
worthlessness, and concentration/decision difficulty. PC1 and PC2 explained
17.9% and 13.9% of item variance. The two pre-specified group centroids were
0.583 apart in the PC1-PC2 plane; the exact four-versus-four label-permutation
probability was 0.057.

PC2 loading correlations with the full result were 0.991 for 2021-2022 and
0.980 for 2023-2024. In the combined symptom-function map, the corresponding
two-dimensional permutation probability was 0.029. Hierarchical clustering
showed weak agreement with the symptom labels (adjusted Rand index 0.16), so
the result supports a continuous contrast rather than recovery of two discrete
symptom classes.

## Function contrast

In the four-domain function map, home management and work loaded on the
positive side of PC2; close relationships and social life loaded on the
negative side. PC1 explained 66.8% of variance and represented common role
impairment. PC2 explained 16.7% and expressed the interpersonal contrast.

The two-cluster solution matched the pre-specified two-versus-two grouping
(adjusted Rand index 1.00), and all within-cluster bootstrap stability values
were 1.00. Split-year PC2 loading correlations exceeded 0.9999. With four
domains, only three substantively different two-versus-two partitions exist;
the smallest attainable exact probability is therefore 0.333. Treat the
separation as descriptive. Home/work are operational proxies for
non-interpersonal function, not pure measures of being alone.

## Software implication

A global two-dimensional map and a focused submap answer different questions.
The combined map emphasized the symptom-function distinction and compressed
the narrower interpersonal contrast. BRIDGE-D therefore lets users create a
local map by passing only the instruments relevant to that contrast.
